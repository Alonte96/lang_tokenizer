The Second International Chinese Word Segmentation Bakeoff data is published at
http://sighan.cs.uchicago.edu/bakeoff2005/ and described there as available for
# research use. The archive's own doc/instructions.txt, however, carries the
original competition terms, which restrict use to that competition and direct
other requests to the original data providers (Academia Sinica, City University
of Hong Kong, Peking University, Microsoft Research Asia).

Two decades of published NLP research treat these corpora as a standard
benchmark. unsegbench follows that practice: it downloads the archive to your
local cache, cites it, and never redistributes it or any derived canonical form.
